1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Couple Games mobile application (the “App”), including renamed, updated or successor versions. Flying Dragon Studio is the public-facing studio brand for this website. The applicable App developer or publisher is identified in the relevant Google Play or Apple App Store listing.

For privacy questions or requests concerning Couple Games, please contact flyingdragonsupport@gmail.com. The relevant App publisher is responsible for the App and its privacy practices as required by applicable law.

2. Information We Process

Couple Games does not require you to create an account. We do not ask you to provide a real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or videos, messages or user-generated content as part of normal gameplay.

Player names, gameplay preferences, settings and game state are generally stored locally on the device. Limited technical, purchase, subscription and paywall-interaction data may be processed by third-party services as explained below.

3. Local Gameplay Data

Information you enter or generate while playing is generally stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • Player names;
  • Game preferences and selected settings;
  • Game state or progress;
  • Other locally stored configuration data.

We do not currently maintain user accounts or a server-side gameplay database for this information. Deleting the App or clearing its locally stored data may permanently remove this information.

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

Depending on platform, location and App version, Couple Games may offer subscriptions, introductory offers, free trials or other in-app purchases. Payments are processed by the distribution platform, such as the Apple App Store or Google Play.

We do not directly receive or store full payment-card or banking information. Apple and Google may independently process account, transaction, subscription and related information under their own privacy notices.

5. Adapty

We use Adapty for subscription management, purchase-status synchronization, premium-access verification, purchase restoration and subscription analytics.

When its SDK is active, Adapty creates an internal profile for the App installation. Couple Games does not require a registered user account, and we do not provide Adapty with a named Couple Games customer account ID. However, purchase and interaction data may be associated with an Adapty profile, app-installation or device identifiers and app-store transaction information.

Depending on your activity and platform, Adapty may process:

  • An Adapty profile identifier and app-installation or device identifiers;
  • Product, transaction and purchase-history identifiers;
  • Subscription access and status;
  • Trial, renewal, cancellation, expiration, billing or refund events;
  • Paywall or subscription-flow impressions and related interactions;
  • Product, price, currency and revenue-related data;
  • Limited technical information necessary to provide the service.

The Adapty SDK is configured to disable Android Advertising ID, Apple IDFA and IP address collection through Adapty’s SDK data-collection settings. We do not intentionally transmit player names, gameplay answers, game prompts, challenge history or other gameplay content to Adapty.

For more information, see Adapty’s Privacy Policy.

6. How We Use Information

Local gameplay information is used to display player names, remember preferences and settings, maintain game state and deliver the selected game experience.

Subscription-related data is used to determine and maintain access, synchronize purchase events, restore eligible purchases, troubleshoot billing, measure paywall impressions and subscription-funnel performance, and analyze trials, conversions, renewals, cancellations, refunds and revenue.

7. Advertising and Tracking

Couple Games does not currently display third-party advertising or include third-party advertising SDKs. We configure Adapty not to collect Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. Analytics, Crash Reporting and Push Notifications

Couple Games does not currently use a general-purpose behavioral or gameplay analytics SDK to track users’ answers or gameplay. Adapty processes subscription-related events and identifiers for subscription management and analytics.

The current App version does not use a third-party crash-reporting SDK or third-party push-notification service. If these practices change, this Policy will be updated where appropriate.

9. Third-Party Services

Third-party services used for app distribution and subscription functionality currently include:

  • Google Play — Android distribution, purchases and subscriptions;
  • Apple App Store — iOS distribution, purchases and subscriptions;
  • Adapty — subscription synchronization, access, restoration and analytics.

These providers may process data under their own privacy policies and legal obligations. Where a provider processes personal data on behalf of the responsible publisher, appropriate contractual and data-protection safeguards must apply.

10. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable data-protection laws require a lawful basis, processing may rely on:

  • Performance of a contract, to provide the App and paid or subscribed features;
  • Legitimate interests, to maintain, secure, troubleshoot and understand the performance of the App and its subscription system;
  • Compliance with applicable legal obligations, including tax, accounting or consumer-protection requirements;
  • Consent, where required by applicable law.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally remains on the device until removed by you, overwritten by the App or deleted along with App data.

App stores and Adapty may retain subscription and transaction-related information as needed to maintain subscription status and entitlement records, analyze subscription performance, resolve billing issues, prevent abuse or meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary by data category, provider requirements and applicable law.

12. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, rectification, deletion, restriction, data portability, objection or information about personal-data processing.

You can contact flyingdragonsupport@gmail.com to exercise applicable rights. Since we do not operate user accounts linked to named identities, relevant purchase or transaction details may be needed to locate an Adapty profile. Please do not send passwords or payment-card numbers.

Local gameplay information can generally be removed by deleting the App or clearing its data. Subscriptions can be managed separately through your Apple or Google account.

13. Children’s Privacy

Couple Games is intended for a mature audience and is not designed for children. Some modes may contain romantic, intimate, suggestive, personal or other mature themes.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. Users should comply with the minimum ages and content ratings applicable in their country and app store.

14. Security and International Processing

We follow a data-minimization approach and design gameplay so that gameplay data can remain on the user’s device. App stores and other service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence.

Where required by law, international transfers must rely on appropriate safeguards. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to the App, ownership or publisher information, data practices, subscription providers, store requirements or applicable law. We will publish updates on this page and update the “Last updated” date.
